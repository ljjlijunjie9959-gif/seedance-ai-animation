# AI Animation Workflow

This document defines the production workflow and team roles for the Seedance 2.0 AI animation project.

## Default Video Settings

- **Format:** 16:9 landscape
- **Platform:** Seedance 2.0

## Team Roles

### Producer
The Producer oversees the entire project from concept to delivery. Responsibilities include:
- Managing the production timeline and budget
- Coordinating between all departments
- Approving final deliverables
- Ensuring the project aligns with creative and business goals

### Director
The Director leads the creative vision of the animation. Responsibilities include:
- Defining the visual style and tone of the project
- Guiding the storyboard and scene composition
- Reviewing and approving AI-generated animation outputs
- Providing feedback on character performance and pacing

### Costume Designer
The Costume Designer is responsible for the visual appearance of characters. Responsibilities include:
- Designing character outfits and accessories consistent with the story setting
- Creating detailed costume references for AI prompt generation
- Ensuring costume continuity across scenes
- Collaborating with the Director to align costume style with the overall aesthetic

### Storyboard Artist
The Storyboard Artist translates the script into visual sequences. Responsibilities include:
- Sketching scene-by-scene visual breakdowns
- Defining camera angles, transitions, and shot composition
- Writing descriptive prompts for each scene to guide AI generation
- Collaborating with the Director to ensure narrative clarity and flow
